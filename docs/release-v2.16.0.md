# Armin Terminal 2.16.0

This release covers the whole market's earnings on EVENTS, fills the revenue estimates of reported quarters, adds every insider's Form 4 trades to MANAGEMENT, switches dates to DD/MM/YYYY by default, and makes watchlists reorderable by drag and drop.

- **Corporate events for the whole market:** EVENTS opens on `14) Market` in the desktop app: every US-listed company reporting in the window above a market value (from $2B by default; $10B, $50B, $200B or any), the coming reports and the last two weeks' results. Each line gives the time of the report, market value, fiscal quarter, EPS and revenue expected and reported, the surprise, and the growth on the year and on the quarter. Clicking a line opens its earnings card: expected against reported for EPS and revenue, beat or miss, and EPS and revenue growth year over year and quarter over quarter. Nasdaq's public earnings calendar gives EPS and market values without a key; Finnhub's and FMP's calendars, with your keys, add revenue and companies Nasdaq leaves out, which take their market value from Nasdaq's stock list. Growth is worked out within one source: Nasdaq's EPS against its calendars of 52 and 13 weeks earlier, FMP's revenue against its own earlier reports; NM marks growth to or from a loss.
- **Revenue expected:** EARNINGS and PROFILE fill the revenue estimates of reported quarters from FMP's earnings history and the market calendars. Yahoo drops a quarter's estimates once it is reported, and Finnhub's free calendar keeps about a month.
- **Management:** each insider's Form 4 filings of the last 12 months from SEC EDGAR: shares bought and sold on the market with their value, shares and options granted by the company, options exercised, shares withheld for tax, the net change and the holding after the latest transaction; the totals of all insiders; and every transaction with its code, price, value, the Rule 10b5-1 flag and a link to the filing. Yahoo's officer list adds the value of unexercised options and, matched by name, each officer's Form 4 holding and 12-month net change.
- **Dates:** SETTINGS (PDF) has *European dates, DD/MM/YYYY*, on by default, also for settings saved by earlier versions. Tables, chart axes, inspected candles, ECO and FRED week labels, received notes and the GP/HP range boxes follow it, and typed dates are read in the chosen order. Bond identifiers keep their US-ordered maturities, as security names do.
- **Watchlists:** drag a line onto another to move it, before or after by where it lands; the arrows work while the list is sorted (the sorted order becomes the manual order) and move past the visible neighbour when the list is filtered. Disabled buttons now look disabled.
- **Readability:** label and value lists keep to a readable width with a faint leader from each label to its value, so full-screen panels no longer leave values far from their labels. P/E on a trailing loss reads NM. A company that pays no dividend says so once. Wide tables drop their least needed columns in half-width panels. The panel's function and security lists draw their arrow in a space of their own, so a long name such as PDF Settings is cut before it rather than under it.

Provider coverage still determines availability: Nasdaq gives no revenue, Finnhub's free calendar covers about a month of past reports, FMP's free plan allows 250 requests a day, and Form 4s need `SEC_USER_AGENT`. Unsupported figures stay unavailable rather than becoming zero or an estimate.

The release workflow runs lint, TypeScript, unit tests, browser tests and Electron tests before building Windows and Linux packages, and publishes only after both native builds and their SHA256 checks succeed. A desktop test that fixed the page clock at noon UTC on 20 October 2026 now dates its IEX trade before that clock, so it no longer fails once that moment has passed.

Local Windows validation: 879 unit tests, 35 browser tests and 30 Electron tests; ESLint, TypeScript and production build. Live Nasdaq, Finnhub, FMP and SEC coverage is not established by these deterministic checks.
