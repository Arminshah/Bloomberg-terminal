# Armin Terminal 2.13.0

- REFRESH button at the right of the top bar (Alt+R): every source the open screens and the background use is asked again now, within each provider's pacing and daily allowance; the button shows the time of the last refresh and, on hover, what was asked, what failed and what was skipped.
- Breaking news about your favourite stocks now stays on screen until you close it or click it, survives a restart, never pops up twice, and the missed ones are counted beside BREAKING and listed on its ★ Favourites tab.
- Paging like the terminal: Page n/m on the function bar, PgUp/PgDn by whole pages, a number before PAGE FWD jumps that many, column headings stay in view.
- Numbered rows and headlines that open with the number and GO on WEI, WEIF, GLCO, CRYP, WB, MOST, EQS and the news screens.
- A mnemonic typed before F1 opens its help; autocomplete shows mnemonics.
- New EXCH (EXCHANGES) screen: exchanges' local time, open or closed, early closes, next open or close.
- New mnemonics ERN, NSE, EQRV, OWN, QR, HVG, PDF and CF; GPO and GPC charts.
- `CHART X 1D` opens the intraday chart and HP takes a range and periodicity (`HP AAPL 1Y W`).

See [terminal-fidelity-v2.13.md](terminal-fidelity-v2.13.md) for what was compared with the Bloomberg Terminal and what remains different.
