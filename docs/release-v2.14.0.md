# Armin Terminal 2.14.0

Includes everything in 2.13.0 (REFRESH button, favourite news pop-ups that stay, paging, numbered rows, EXCH), whose release build stopped on a slow test start, plus a second pass at matching the Bloomberg Terminal:

- The red function bar laid out as the terminal's: security at the left, numbered actions, page count, title and mnemonic at the right.
- GP: white price line over a blue mountain, last price tagged, amber range boxes that take typed dates.
- DES: Profile, Issue Info, Ratios, Revenue & EPS and Industry Info pages with numbered boxes that open functions.
- HP: the price table by weekday in blocks, with Range, Period and View fields; `HP AAPL OHLC` for the OHLC view.
- Monitors (WEI, WEIF, GLCO, WB) number each region from its own ten; WB grouped by region.
- Headlines as number, headline, source and time; ERN, ANR, ECO, FA, MENU, MOST, HDS and RV laid out as the terminal shows them.
- New FOMC screen, RSI, MACD and BOLL study commands, EVT and ECMX.

See [terminal-fidelity-v2.14.md](terminal-fidelity-v2.14.md) for what was compared and what remains different.
